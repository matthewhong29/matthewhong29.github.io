D.R.E.A.M.S.

Co-Lead · Mechanical & Structural · Planning & Communications

Data Recovery and Environmental Assessment for Marine Surveillance. A fully autonomous unmanned surface vehicle (USV) developed to collect environmental data in degraded aquatic ecosystems. Built as a two-person first-year project within a single semester on a £100 budget. Features a custom waterproof sensor pod, 1km verified wireless range, three dedicated Raspberry Pi Pico microcontrollers (vessel, sensor pod, and shore station), comprehensive fault-tolerant safety systems, and structured data output formatted for Excel import. Components were modeled in SolidWorks with bilateral tolerances for 3D printing, including a Van Veen grab sediment sampler delivered with a complete engineering drawing package and BOM.

Magnetic Fishing Rod Vest Clip

Designer & Builder · Solo Project

A DIY magnetic quick-release system for attaching a fishing rod to a vest — eliminating the need to set the rod down between casts. The vest-side housing features a D-ring loop and ribbed internal gusset structure for load transfer. The rod-side clamp uses four curved spring fingers that flex to snap around the rod blank and retain it under load. Both housings share a dual N52 neodymium magnet base spaced to resist rotation and provide predictable release. Epoxy-coated magnets selected for corrosion resistance in outdoor environments.

CAD Design 3D Printing (ASA) Magnet Engineering Hardware Design
