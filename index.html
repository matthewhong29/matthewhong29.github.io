Golf Practice Net

Designer & Builder · Solo Project

A 10×7×3 ft freestanding golf practice net designed from the ground up. Features a custom PVC frame with 3D-printed ASA structural joints designed for UV and weather resistance, and commercial-grade #21 knotted nylon netting. Every joint and connection point was designed in CAD before fabrication to ensure structural integrity under repeated impact loading.
